Ye Jin

Materials Science · Computer Science · Neuroengineering

I’m a materials science researcher with a background in computer science and machine learning. I like working at the interface where soft, biocompatible materials meet algorithms and neural circuits.

Long‑term, I aim to contribute to curative therapies for neurological disorders such as Parkinson’s disease and Alzheimer’s by designing better bioelectronic interfaces and data‑driven models of the brain.

I enjoy turning complex ideas into experiments and systems — from flexible neural interfaces and micro‑/nanorobotics to on‑device learning and simulation.

Based in Munich, Germany.

Research Interests

A snapshot of the directions I’m currently most excited about.

Soft Bioelectronic Interfaces

Flexible, transparent, and biocompatible sensor platforms that can stably record and modulate neural activity without damaging tissue.

Brain Modeling

Linking device physics and material properties to circuit‑level neural dynamics, to understand how interventions propagate through the brain.

Computational Materials Design

Using simulation and data‑driven search to discover materials tailored for neural interfaces, drug delivery, and neuromorphic electronics.

2D Materials

Exploring graphene and other 2D materials as ultra‑thin, high‑performance platforms for sensing and bioelectronics in the brain.
